# PHASE 123 — INSTITUTIONAL ADOPTION LAYER

## OBJECTIVE
Prepare the platform for adoption by large enterprises, institutional buyers, and regulated operating environments.

## CORE OUTCOME
Establish procurement-ready, governance-ready, and audit-grade institutional operating baselines.

## COMPONENTS

### 1. Institutional Readiness Model
- enterprise onboarding packs
- procurement and due-diligence support
- institutional deployment checklists
- approval authority mapping
- operating responsibility model

### 2. Governance-Grade Controls
- institutional role hierarchies
- approval delegation chains
- policy exception handling
- audit evidence exports
- governance attestation workflow

### 3. Regulated Operating Readiness
- sensitive workflow classification
- higher-assurance control paths
- retention and export discipline
- external audit support model
- compliance evidence packaging

### 4. Buyer Assurance Layer
- board and executive readiness packs
- security and governance posture summaries
- operating model documentation
- trust and evidence assurance baseline

## EVENTS
INSTITUTIONAL_PROFILE_CREATED
INSTITUTIONAL_CONTROL_MODEL_ASSIGNED
GOVERNANCE_ATTESTATION_ISSUED
INSTITUTIONAL_EXPORT_GENERATED
REGULATED_WORKFLOW_CLASSIFIED

## GOVERNANCE
- institutional controls must be explicit and reviewable
- regulated workflow paths must be separately classified
- attestations and exports must be reproducible and auditable
- exception handling must be approved and logged
