# QUALIFIED INTAKE AND CONVERSION PROTOCOL

## Purpose

This protocol defines how external interactions are qualified, classified, and converted into downstream governed workflows.

## Protocol Principle

No external interaction becomes pipeline, diligence, or activation work unless it passes qualified intake.

Qualified intake must determine:

- who is engaging
- why they are engaging
- what class of interaction exists
- whether the interaction is permitted
- how the interaction is routed

## Intake Stages

### Stage 1 — Source classification
Identify audience class and legitimacy.

### Stage 2 — Intent classification
Determine whether intent is commercial, institutional, sovereign, investor, partner, or exploratory.

### Stage 3 — Boundary validation
Confirm disclosure and engagement limits.

### Stage 4 — Qualification decision
Approve, hold, escalate, or reject.

### Stage 5 — Downstream conversion
Map qualified interaction into the correct governed system pathway.

## Protocol Outputs

Each intake record must produce:

- source class
- intent class
- qualification status
- conversion path
- escalation requirement
- traceability reference
- closure status

## Fail-Closed Rule

No intake may convert into active institutional workflow if classification, boundary validation, or routing is unresolved.
