# PARTNER AND INVESTOR INTERACTION MODEL

## Purpose

This document defines how WorkCaptain / ProWork structures partner and investor interaction under governed institutional rules.

## Interaction Principle

Partner and investor interaction must remain confidence-safe, traceable, and classification-aware.

No interaction may create implied commitments, implied approvals, or ungoverned disclosure.

## Interaction Classes

### 1. Investor confidence interaction
Used for investor orientation, assurance review, governance maturity, and institutional confidence building.

### 2. Diligence interaction
Used for structured diligence review within approved disclosure and escalation constraints.

### 3. Strategic partner interaction
Used for capability alignment, co-delivery exploration, channel design, or market access structure.

### 4. Activation interaction
Used when a partner or investor interaction becomes actionable and requires downstream governed routing.

## Required Controls

Every interaction must define:

- audience class
- approved scope
- disclosure class
- escalation conditions
- conversion criteria
- traceability record

## Rule

Interaction confidence must not exceed operating truth, and interaction scope must not exceed approved boundaries.
