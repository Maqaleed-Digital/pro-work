# SOVEREIGN MARKET ACTIVATION SYSTEM

## Purpose

This document defines how WorkCaptain / ProWork activates the market in a sovereign-compatible, institutionally governed manner.

The system converts sovereign relevance into structured activation logic without compromising evidence, governance, or disclosure discipline.

## Activation Principle

Sovereign market activation must not be campaign-led only.

It must be governed by:

- market entry classification
- sovereign relevance assessment
- enterprise and institutional fit
- portfolio and allocation compatibility
- resilience and reserve compatibility
- disclosure and engagement boundaries

## Sovereign Activation Domains

The system should prioritize activation across:

1. Workforce modernization
2. evidence-first compliance systems
3. AI-native institutional operating infrastructure
4. enterprise execution governance
5. KSA-first and GCC-scalable sovereign-tech positioning
6. public-private institutional readiness pathways

## Activation Outputs

Each sovereign activation record must produce:

- activation domain
- target institutional class
- strategic relevance view
- market readiness status
- interaction pathway
- governance sensitivity view
- escalation or sponsorship requirement

## Strategic Guardrail

Sovereign relevance may increase activation priority, but may not bypass governance, allocation, resilience, or disclosure controls.
