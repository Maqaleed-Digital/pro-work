# EXECUTION IDENTITY AND PERMANENCE RULES

## Purpose

This document defines the execution identity and permanence rules of WorkCaptain / ProWork as an institution.

## Identity Principle

The institution is defined by its operating doctrine, not by temporary labels, campaigns, or individuals.

Its execution identity is evidence-first, governance-bound, sovereign-compatible, and continuity-driven.

## Permanence Rules

The institution must preserve the following identity rules:

1. Truth precedes narrative
2. governance precedes speed
3. validity precedes confidence
4. discipline precedes exposure
5. continuity precedes personality
6. sovereign compatibility requires structural credibility
7. every external interface remains subordinate to internal truth

## Identity Rule

Any future expansion, repositioning, market activation, or strategic evolution must remain compatible with these permanence rules.

## Outcome

Execution identity and permanence rules convert the operating stack into a durable institutional doctrine.
