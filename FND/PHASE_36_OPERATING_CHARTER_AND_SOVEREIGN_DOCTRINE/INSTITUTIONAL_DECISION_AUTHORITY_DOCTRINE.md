# INSTITUTIONAL DECISION AUTHORITY DOCTRINE

## Purpose

This document defines how decision authority is permanently structured across the institution.

## Doctrine Principle

Authority must remain structured, bounded, and traceable.

Authority is not legitimacy by itself.
Legitimacy comes from authority operating inside governed constraints.

## Authority Levels

### Level 1 — System authority
Enforces standing controls, invalidity rules, traceability, and fail-closed behavior.

### Level 2 — Executive authority
Directs operating decisions within board-approved and charter-compatible boundaries.

### Level 3 — Board authority
Defines institutional posture, governing overrides, strategic boundaries, and enduring control commitments.

## Doctrine Rules

- System authority enforces institutional rules without discretionary weakening
- Executive authority cannot override board-defined standing constraints
- Board authority cannot legitimize false evidence or fabricated validity
- All authority exercise must remain traceable and doctrine-compatible

## Outcome

Decision authority becomes permanent institutional doctrine rather than ad hoc power.
