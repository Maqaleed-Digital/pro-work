# LONG HORIZON CONTINUITY AND SUCCESSION LOGIC

## Purpose

This document defines how WorkCaptain / ProWork preserves continuity across time, leadership changes, strategy cycles, and institutional scale expansion.

## Continuity Principle

The institution must outlive any single operator, executive cycle, or engagement phase.

Continuity must be preserved through governed systems, not personal memory.

## Continuity Requirements

The institution must preserve:

1. source-of-truth continuity
2. evidence continuity
3. governance continuity
4. allocation continuity
5. resilience continuity
6. disclosure continuity
7. engagement continuity

## Succession Logic

Leadership transition or delegation must not reset institutional truth.

Successors inherit:

- charter commitments
- standing constraints
- governance stack
- evidence lineage obligations
- sovereign execution doctrine
- continuity responsibilities

## Rule

No succession or delegation event may suspend the institutional control stack or downgrade evidence-backed operating discipline.

## Outcome

Continuity and succession logic formalize permanence beyond founder or operator dependence.
