# NON-NEGOTIABLE INSTITUTIONAL CONSTRAINTS

## Purpose

This document defines the permanent non-negotiable constraints governing institutional behavior.

## Constraint Principle

Constraints are not optional recommendations.
They are standing validity boundaries.

## Constraint Set

### Constraint 1 — Phase 26 root truth integrity is mandatory
All downstream validity depends on fully resolved and hashed evidence integrity.

### Constraint 2 — Board governance remains binding
Board-approved governance remains superior to executive preference.

### Constraint 3 — Allocation discipline remains binding
No capital, priority, or strategic allocation may bypass governed scoring, reallocation, or exposure rules.

### Constraint 4 — Resilience discipline remains binding
No scenario, reserve, or contingency response may bypass resilience controls.

### Constraint 5 — Disclosure discipline remains binding
No outward confidence claim may exceed operating truth.

### Constraint 6 — Engagement discipline remains binding
No market-facing activity may bypass classification, qualification, routing, or traceability.

### Constraint 7 — Sovereign compatibility does not override discipline
Sovereign importance may increase relevance, but may not bypass institutional controls.

## Invalidity Rule

Any action conflicting with these constraints is institutionally invalid and must not be treated as legitimate execution.
