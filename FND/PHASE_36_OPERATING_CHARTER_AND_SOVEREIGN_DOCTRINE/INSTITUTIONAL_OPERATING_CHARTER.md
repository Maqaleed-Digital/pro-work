# INSTITUTIONAL OPERATING CHARTER

## Purpose

This document defines the permanent institutional operating charter for WorkCaptain / ProWork.

The charter formalizes the governing purpose, operating identity, structural commitments, and continuity obligations of the institution.

The charter is not a strategy note.
It is the standing institutional operating basis.

## Charter Principle

The institution must operate through governed continuity, not personality-dependent momentum.

All institutional behavior must remain subordinate to:

- validated truth integrity
- board-governed authority
- allocation discipline
- resilience discipline
- disclosure discipline
- engagement discipline
- sovereign execution compatibility

## Charter Commitments

The institution is committed to:

1. evidence-first operating truth
2. fail-closed governance
3. bounded institutional authority
4. sovereign-compatible execution discipline
5. traceable decision logic
6. durable continuity across leadership cycles
7. external confidence grounded in internal truth

## Charter Rule

No institutional action is valid if it conflicts with the charter commitments or attempts to bypass the standing control stack established in prior phases.

## Charter Outcome

The institutional operating charter establishes the permanent governing identity of the system.
