# WORKCAPTAIN / PROWORK — SOVEREIGN / REGULATORY POSITIONING DECLARATION

Version: 1.0
Status: ACTIVE

## Purpose

Declare the sovereign and regulatory positioning posture of WorkCaptain / ProWork in support of jurisdiction-aware and evidence-first platform operations.

## Positioning Statements

- sovereign-by-design policy control posture
- jurisdiction-aware isolation and governance
- evidence-first operational model
- human-in-the-loop protection for high-impact actions
- configurable policy evolution and controlled change
- reviewable and exportable evidence artifacts

## Boundary Statement

This declaration describes platform posture and design intent.
It does not claim regulator approval, statutory endorsement, or legal interpretation.

- strategic partner discussions
- sovereign tech positioning
- institutional narrative and board material
