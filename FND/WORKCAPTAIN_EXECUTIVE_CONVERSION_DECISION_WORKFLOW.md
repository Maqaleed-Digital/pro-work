# WORKCAPTAIN / PROWORK — EXECUTIVE CONVERSION DECISION WORKFLOW

Version: 1.0
Status: ACTIVE

## Workflow

1. confirm stage
2. confirm owner
3. confirm forecast and confidence posture
4. confirm concentration and dependency posture
5. confirm evidence basis
6. decide:
   - continue
   - reprioritize
   - intervene
   - escalate
   - pause
   - park
   - close_won
   - close_lost

## Rule

No executive conversion decision may be logged without stage, owner, portfolio posture, and evidence basis.
