# WORKCAPTAIN / PROWORK — PIPELINE STAGE MODEL

Version: 1.0
Status: ACTIVE

## Allowed Stages

- identified
- qualified
- activated
- diligence
- proposal
- negotiation
- decision
- won
- lost
- parked

## Stage Rules

- identified: opportunity exists but not yet qualified
- qualified: fit, owner, and pursuit category confirmed
- activated: live outreach or engagement approved
- diligence: stakeholder review and artifact exchange active
- proposal: structured commercial or strategic proposal active
- negotiation: deal terms under active discussion
- decision: final go / no-go state pending closure
- won: outcome secured
- lost: outcome not secured
- parked: intentionally paused without closure

## Rule

Every stage transition must be explicit, logged, and owner-accountable.
