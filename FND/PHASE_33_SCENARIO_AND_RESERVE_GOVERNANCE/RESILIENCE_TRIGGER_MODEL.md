# RESILIENCE TRIGGER MODEL

## Purpose

This document defines the resilience triggers that activate scenario review, reserve review, or contingency action.

## Trigger Types

### 1. Revenue trigger
Activated when conversion quality, realization confidence, or revenue posture deteriorates materially.

### 2. Concentration trigger
Activated when exposure becomes too dependent on a narrow set of opportunities, sectors, or institutional paths.

### 3. Dependency trigger
Activated when portfolio continuity relies excessively on a fragile set of actors, approvals, cycles, or channels.

### 4. Governance trigger
Activated when board constraints, evidence integrity, or escalation discipline are weakened.

### 5. Sovereign trigger
Activated when sovereign timing, regulatory relevance, public-private openings, or strategic national alignment materially changes.

## Trigger Outputs

Each trigger must produce:

- trigger type
- severity view
- impacted portfolio layer
- scenario review requirement
- reserve review requirement
- board relevance flag
- intervention priority

## Rule

Triggers do not authorize action by themselves.

They authorize governed review.

All resulting action remains subordinate to evidence validity, governance constraints, and allocation discipline.
