# SCENARIO MODEL DEFINITIONS

## Purpose

This document defines the controlled scenario models used by the institutional planning framework.

## Scenario 1 — Downside

Represents contraction, slippage, delay, dependency stress, or conversion weakness.

Typical characteristics:
- reduced conversion velocity
- higher intervention load
- higher concentration sensitivity
- greater reserve preservation need
- tighter executive and board oversight

## Scenario 2 — Base

Represents the expected governed path under current portfolio assumptions.

Typical characteristics:
- stable conversion profile
- controlled allocation posture
- normal reserve requirements
- standard intervention cadence
- board oversight under normal rhythm

## Scenario 3 — Upside

Represents stronger-than-expected conversion, acceleration, or portfolio unlocking.

Typical characteristics:
- higher growth opportunity
- increased allocation pressure
- controlled scale reinforcement need
- possible reserve deployment decision
- board review for acceleration discipline

## Scenario 4 — Sovereign Opportunity Acceleration

Represents strategic acceleration triggered by sovereign-aligned openings, partnerships, public-private alignment, labor-market modernization demand, or institutional timing windows.

Typical characteristics:
- high strategic relevance
- elevated board visibility
- strict governance and discipline requirements
- reserve redeployment review
- sensitivity to execution credibility

## Scenario 5 — Portfolio Stress

Represents a condition where concentration, dependency, evidence instability, or governance strain materially affects institutional posture.

Typical characteristics:
- resilience deterioration
- immediate board attention
- mandatory intervention review
- reserve protection or deployment decision
- structural reallocation requirement

## Model Rule

Scenarios are not predictions.
They are governed preparedness models.

No scenario may be treated as valid unless it is:

- evidence-compatible
- governance-compatible
- allocation-compatible
- resilience-aware
