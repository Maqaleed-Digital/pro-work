# WORKCAPTAIN / PROWORK — INVESTOR PURSUIT PLAYBOOK

Version: 1.0
Status: ACTIVE

## Objective

Guide investor pursuit from activation to decision using governed artifacts.

## Steps

1. identify investor target
2. qualify fit and narrative relevance
3. activate outreach using approved investor materials
4. enter diligence using certification and readiness artifacts
5. advance to proposal or decision
6. record outcome and next state

## Rule

Investor pursuit may emphasize platform category, governance maturity, and readiness posture, but may not imply unsupported financial or regulatory outcomes.
