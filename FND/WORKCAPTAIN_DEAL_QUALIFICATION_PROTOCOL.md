# WORKCAPTAIN / PROWORK — DEAL QUALIFICATION PROTOCOL

Version: 1.0
Status: ACTIVE

## Qualification Criteria

An opportunity may be marked qualified only if all are true:

- named stakeholder or account exists
- pursuit category is set
- owner is assigned
- strategic fit is stated
- approved artifact basis is identified
- assurance boundary check is complete
- next action is defined

## Blocking Conditions

Qualification must fail if:

- no owner
- no artifact basis
- unsupported claims are required to advance
- no next action exists
