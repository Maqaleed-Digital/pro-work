# WORKCAPTAIN / PROWORK — ACTIVE PIPELINE DECISION WORKFLOW

Version: 1.0
Status: ACTIVE

## Decision Workflow

1. confirm current stage
2. confirm artifact basis
3. confirm assurance boundary
4. confirm owner and next action
5. decide:
   - proceed
   - pause
   - escalate
   - park
   - close_won
   - close_lost

## Rule

No decision may be recorded without stage, owner, and artifact basis.
