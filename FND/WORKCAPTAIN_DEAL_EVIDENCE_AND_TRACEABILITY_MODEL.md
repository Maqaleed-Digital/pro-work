# WORKCAPTAIN / PROWORK — DEAL EVIDENCE AND TRACEABILITY MODEL

Version: 1.0
Status: ACTIVE

## Required Traceability Elements

Each live opportunity record must include:

- opportunity identifier
- pursuit category
- stage
- owner
- approved artifact references
- diligence questions and responses
- decision points
- escalation events
- outcome state

## Rule

No commercial, investor, or strategic statement may exist without traceability back to approved artifacts and recorded stage context.
