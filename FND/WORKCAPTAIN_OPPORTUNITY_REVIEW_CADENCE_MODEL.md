# WORKCAPTAIN / PROWORK — OPPORTUNITY REVIEW CADENCE MODEL

Version: 1.0
Status: ACTIVE

## Review Cadence

- daily: owner next action check
- weekly: stage and momentum review
- milestone-based: diligence, proposal, negotiation review
- decision-based: close or escalate

## Rule

No active opportunity may skip review cadence without being paused, parked, or escalated.
