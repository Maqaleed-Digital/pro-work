# TRUST SEAL INVALIDITY AND REVOCATION RULES

## Purpose

This document defines when trust-seal status becomes invalid and how revocation is recognized.

## Revocation Principle

Trust-seal status is not permanent by declaration.
It is permanent only while sustaining conditions remain valid.

## Invalidity Conditions

Trust-seal status must be treated as invalid when any of the following occur:

1. Phase 26 truth dependency breaks
2. shared doctrine becomes materially incomplete
3. federation-wide accountability becomes non-enforceable
4. command coherence materially degrades
5. disclosure or sovereign conduct materially breaks institutional credibility
6. closure assurance becomes contradicted by actual operating state

## Revocation Outputs

Each revocation review must define:

- invalidity condition triggered
- scope of affected certification
- trust-seal status
- restoration prerequisites
- escalation requirement
- closure record

## Revocation Rule

No trust-seal status may remain active while material invalidity conditions are known and unresolved.

## Outcome

Invalidity and revocation rules ensure the trust seal remains governed truth, not ceremonial status.
