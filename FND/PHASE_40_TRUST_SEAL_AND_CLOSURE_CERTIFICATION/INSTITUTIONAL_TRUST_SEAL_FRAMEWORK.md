# INSTITUTIONAL TRUST SEAL FRAMEWORK

## Purpose

This document defines the institutional trust seal framework for WorkCaptain / ProWork.

The trust seal formalizes the highest institutional confidence state that may be recognized once the full control stack, doctrine stack, federation stack, and accountability stack are validated as complete, traceable, and enforceable.

The trust seal is not branding.
It is a governed certification state.

## Trust Seal Principle

A trust seal may exist only when confidence is fully subordinate to validated institutional truth.

The trust seal must be grounded in:

- evidence integrity
- governance integrity
- allocation integrity
- resilience integrity
- disclosure integrity
- engagement integrity
- doctrine integrity
- federation integrity
- accountability integrity

## Trust Seal Outputs

The framework must define:

- trust seal eligibility status
- trust seal scope
- trust seal dependency set
- trust seal sustaining conditions
- trust seal invalidity conditions
- trust seal revocation relevance

## Trust Seal Rule

No institution, entity, or federation may claim trust-seal status if any required control layer remains incomplete, invalid, or only narratively asserted.

## Outcome

The institutional trust seal framework establishes the governed basis for formal institutional closure confidence.
