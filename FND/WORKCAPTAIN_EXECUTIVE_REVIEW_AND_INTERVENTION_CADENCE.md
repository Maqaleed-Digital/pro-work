# WORKCAPTAIN / PROWORK — EXECUTIVE REVIEW AND INTERVENTION CADENCE

Version: 1.0
Status: ACTIVE

## Cadence

- weekly: executive portfolio review
- milestone-based: proposal, negotiation, and decision-window intervention review
- exception-based: concentration, dependency, or escalation review
- closure-based: won / lost portfolio learning review

## Rule

No executive intervention may remain unreviewed outside cadence.
