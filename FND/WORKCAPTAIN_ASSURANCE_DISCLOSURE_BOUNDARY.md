# WORKCAPTAIN / PROWORK — ASSURANCE DISCLOSURE BOUNDARY

Version: 1.0
Status: ACTIVE

## Allowed Statements

- certified internal governance state
- evidence-backed production governance seal
- enterprise assurance posture
- sovereign positioning posture
- institutional deployment readiness
- certification maintenance protocol exists

## Disallowed Statements

- regulator approved
- legally certified
- externally audited unless separately obtained
- universally compliant across all jurisdictions
- risk free
- endorsed by sovereign authority unless separately obtained

## Rule

All external disclosure must remain within this boundary.
