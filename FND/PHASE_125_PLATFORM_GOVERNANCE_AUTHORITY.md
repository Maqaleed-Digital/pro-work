# PHASE 125 — PLATFORM GOVERNANCE AUTHORITY

## OBJECTIVE
Establish the platform as a formal authority layer for execution governance, trust enforcement, policy discipline, and institutional operating standards.

## CORE OUTCOME
Move from governed platform to governance-defining operating infrastructure.

## COMPONENTS

### 1. Governance Authority Model
- platform-wide policy authority
- control standard registry
- mandatory gate framework
- exception governance board
- authority escalation model

### 2. Standards System
- execution standard definitions
- evidence standard definitions
- trust and approval standards
- AI governance standards
- institutional control standards

### 3. Assurance and Certification
- governance certification baseline
- tenant governance maturity profiles
- operating-standard readiness packs
- internal assurance scorecards
- certification lifecycle model

### 4. Authority Exports
- governance posture exports
- standard compliance reports
- certification summaries
- board readiness authority packs
- external assurance support artifacts

## EVENTS
GOVERNANCE_STANDARD_PUBLISHED
CONTROL_AUTHORITY_ASSIGNED
CERTIFICATION_PROFILE_CREATED
GOVERNANCE_EXCEPTION_APPROVED
AUTHORITY_EXPORT_GENERATED

## GOVERNANCE
- all standards must be versioned and published under explicit authority
- exceptions must be approved and permanently traceable
- certification and assurance outputs must map to underlying evidence
- authority layer must not bypass core trust and approval controls
