# PHASE 122 — AI-NATIVE ENTERPRISE MODE

## OBJECTIVE
Formalize enterprise operating mode where human teams and AI operators collaborate within governed workflows, bounded autonomy, and policy-controlled execution.

## CORE OUTCOME
Enable organizations to run AI-native operating models safely, observably, and at scale.

## COMPONENTS

### 1. AI-Native Operating Model
- AI-first workflow profiles
- mixed human + AI execution teams
- AI role assignment models
- operator utilization controls
- approval-aware autonomy boundaries

### 2. Enterprise Policy Profiles
- model policy assignments
- AI usage tier controls
- execution risk classifications
- domain-specific AI constraints
- enterprise approval modes

### 3. AI Workforce Coordination
- AI operator roster
- workload balancing
- operator specialization mapping
- execution supervision model
- human escalation routing

### 4. Enterprise Telemetry
- AI utilization metrics
- human override rates
- approval latency
- execution quality scoring
- policy violation detection

## EVENTS
AI_ENTERPRISE_PROFILE_CREATED
AI_OPERATOR_POOL_ASSIGNED
AI_USAGE_POLICY_APPLIED
AI_OVERRIDE_RECORDED
AI_ENTERPRISE_EXECUTION_PROFILE_UPDATED

## GOVERNANCE
- no high-impact autonomous action without explicit boundary policy
- all AI execution must be observable and reviewable
- enterprise AI behavior must remain policy-bound and versioned
- human override must remain available for all critical workflows
