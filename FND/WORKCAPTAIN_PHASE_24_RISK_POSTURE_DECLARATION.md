# WORKCAPTAIN / PROWORK — PHASE 24 RISK POSTURE DECLARATION

Version: 1.0
Status: ACTIVE

## Declared Risk Posture

The platform is declared production-ready within a governed, fail-closed, evidence-backed boundary.

## Statements

- Critical controls must remain continuously enforced
- Residual risk is acknowledged and managed, not denied
- High-impact actions remain human-approved where required
- Certification is invalid if evidence continuity is broken
- Certification is invalid if control degradation is detected
- Certification reflects platform governance posture, not universal legal clearance

## Outcome Classes

- pending: prerequisites not yet fully evaluated
- certified: all phase 24 validation conditions passed
- blocked: one or more validation conditions failed
