# CAPITAL GOVERNANCE CONSTRAINTS

## Purpose

This document defines the non-negotiable governance constraints for all Phase 32 allocation decisions.

## Constraint Set

### Constraint 1 — Evidence validity required
All allocation decisions must inherit validated evidence lineage.

### Constraint 2 — Phase 26 root validity enforced
If Phase 26 evidence integrity fails, allocation validity fails.

### Constraint 3 — Phase 31 institutional governance enforced
If board governance constraints are missing, allocation decisions are invalid.

### Constraint 4 — Concentration exposure constrained
No allocation may intensify concentration beyond board-tolerated exposure without explicit board approval.

### Constraint 5 — Dependency exposure constrained
No allocation may increase dependency fragility without explicit rationale and governance approval.

### Constraint 6 — Sovereign alignment cannot bypass discipline
Sovereign positioning may strengthen priority but may not bypass evidence, governance, or resilience logic.

### Constraint 7 — Board override remains final
Board-approved capital and portfolio directives override executive allocation preferences.

## Fail-Closed Rule

If any required constraint is unresolved, the allocation output is invalid and must not be treated as executable institutional direction.
