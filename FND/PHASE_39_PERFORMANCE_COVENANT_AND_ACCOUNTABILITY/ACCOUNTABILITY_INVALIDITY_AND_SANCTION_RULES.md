# ACCOUNTABILITY INVALIDITY AND SANCTION RULES

## Purpose

This document defines invalidity and sanction rules for entities that materially violate the institutional performance covenant.

## Invalidity Principle

Invalidity exists when an entity's conduct materially breaks the conditions required for trusted participation in the federated institutional system.

## Invalidity Conditions

An entity must be treated as invalid for standing purposes when any of the following remain unresolved:

1. repeated truth integrity breach
2. repeated governance non-compliance
3. doctrine-incompatible operating conduct
4. concealed escalation or breach status
5. disclosure or engagement conduct damaging confidence integrity
6. sovereign-sensitive conduct harming portfolio credibility
7. failed remediation after enforced review

## Sanction Classes

### Class 1 — Controlled standing restriction
Applies when recovery is possible and shared damage remains limited.

### Class 2 — Federated participation restriction
Applies when shared portfolio risk is material and local containment is insufficient.

### Class 3 — Institutional enforcement action
Applies when doctrine integrity, confidence architecture, or sovereign credibility is materially threatened.

## Rule

Sanctions must remain traceable, doctrine-compatible, and proportional to shared institutional risk.

## Outcome

Invalidity and sanction rules protect the federated portfolio from persistent performance and accountability degradation.
