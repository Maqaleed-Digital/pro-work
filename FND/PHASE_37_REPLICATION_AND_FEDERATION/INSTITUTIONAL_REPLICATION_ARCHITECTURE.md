# INSTITUTIONAL REPLICATION ARCHITECTURE

## Purpose

This document defines how WorkCaptain / ProWork replicates its institutional operating model across new entities, ventures, subsidiaries, or portfolio vehicles.

Replication is not cloning by convenience.
It is governed inheritance of a validated operating doctrine.

## Architecture Principle

Any replicated entity must inherit the institutional control stack rather than reinterpret it.

Replication must preserve:

- truth integrity
- governance integrity
- allocation integrity
- resilience integrity
- disclosure integrity
- engagement integrity
- doctrine integrity

## Replication Classes

The replication architecture must support at minimum:

1. wholly controlled entity replication
2. venture or subsidiary replication
3. joint-venture style controlled replication
4. sovereign-sensitive replicated operating unit
5. experimental but governed entity extension

## Replication Outputs

Each replication design must define:

- entity class
- replication scope
- inherited control layers
- local adaptation boundaries
- escalation linkage
- source-of-truth linkage
- invalidity conditions

## Replication Rule

No entity may claim institutional inheritance unless the required control layers and continuity obligations are explicitly inherited and preserved.

## Outcome

Replication architecture ensures scale occurs through governed duplication of doctrine, not dilution of doctrine.
