# CROSS-ENTITY SOVEREIGN OPERATING DISCIPLINE

## Purpose

This document defines how sovereign-compatible execution discipline is preserved across multiple entities in the federated portfolio.

## Discipline Principle

Sovereign compatibility must remain consistent across entities, even when local execution differs.

Cross-entity sovereign discipline requires:

- common doctrine inheritance
- shared credibility thresholds
- controlled local variation
- common disclosure posture
- portfolio escalation when sovereign sensitivity increases

## Discipline Requirements

All entities operating in sovereign-sensitive contexts must preserve:

1. KSA-first structural credibility
2. GCC-scalable readiness where applicable
3. policy-aware execution conduct
4. compliance-first posture
5. bounded public and institutional engagement
6. common truth and disclosure discipline

## Cross-Entity Rule

No local entity may claim sovereign-grade legitimacy while violating shared institutional doctrine.

## Outcome

Cross-entity sovereign discipline preserves credibility across the federated portfolio.
