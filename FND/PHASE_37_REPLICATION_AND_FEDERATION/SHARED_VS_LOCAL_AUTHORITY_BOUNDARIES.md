# SHARED VS LOCAL AUTHORITY BOUNDARIES

## Purpose

This document defines which forms of authority remain shared at the portfolio level and which may be exercised locally by replicated or federated entities.

## Boundary Principle

Local flexibility is permitted only inside shared institutional constraints.

Authority must always remain:

- bounded
- traceable
- doctrine-compatible
- escalation-compatible

## Shared Authority Domains

These remain shared and must not be localized without explicit federation doctrine:

1. truth integrity rules
2. invalidity rules
3. board-governed strategic constraints
4. capital exposure ceilings
5. disclosure discipline
6. sovereign execution doctrine
7. cross-entity escalation rules

## Local Authority Domains

These may be localized within approved boundaries:

1. entity-specific execution priorities
2. local operating cadence
3. entity-specific engagement routing
4. market-facing adaptation within approved boundaries
5. local resource deployment inside approved allocation limits

## Boundary Rule

Local authority ends where shared institutional validity begins.

## Outcome

The authority boundary model prevents doctrine drift while allowing controlled local execution.
