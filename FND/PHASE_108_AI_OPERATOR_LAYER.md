# PHASE 108 — AI OPERATOR LAYER

## OBJECTIVE
Introduce bounded AI operators executing workflows.

## AI OPERATOR TYPES

- analytics operator
- execution operator
- compliance operator

## CAPABILITIES

- execute playbooks
- generate artifacts
- escalate to human

## CONSTRAINTS

- cannot perform irreversible actions
- must log all activity

## EVENTS

AI_OPERATOR_ASSIGNED
AI_OPERATOR_EXECUTED
AI_OPERATOR_ESCALATED

## GOVERNANCE

- full audit trail
- human override required
