## Workflow

1. confirm opportunity stage = diligence
2. confirm approved artifact set
3. confirm disclosure boundary
4. answer only with bounded approved statements
5. record requests, responses, and next actions
6. escalate unsupported asks before answering
7. close diligence or move to proposal / decision

## Rule

No diligence response may be delivered without artifact basis and disclosure-boundary compliance.
