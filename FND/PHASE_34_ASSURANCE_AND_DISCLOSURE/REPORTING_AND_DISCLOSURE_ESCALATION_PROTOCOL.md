# REPORTING AND DISCLOSURE ESCALATION PROTOCOL

## Purpose

This protocol defines when assurance reporting or external disclosure must be escalated for institutional review.

## Escalation Triggers

Escalation is mandatory when any of the following occur:

- classification conflict
- open validity conflict
- unresolved governance issue
- sensitive allocation posture
- sensitive reserve posture
- active strategic escalation
- disclosure ambiguity
- external request beyond approved boundary

## Escalation Levels

### Level 1 — Reporting review
Used when pack structure, classification, or validity mapping needs correction.

### Level 2 — Executive disclosure review
Used when external disclosure may affect institutional posture or confidence quality.

### Level 3 — Board disclosure escalation
Used when the disclosure touches strategic sensitivity, capital posture, sovereign positioning, or major confidence risk.

## Protocol Outputs

Each escalation review must produce:

- trigger identified
- escalation level
- impacted audience
- affected disclosure boundary
- approval requirement
- resolution status
- closure record

## Fail-Closed Rule

No disputed disclosure may be released while escalation remains unresolved.
