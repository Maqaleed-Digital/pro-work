# ASSURANCE REPORTING PACK STRUCTURE

## Purpose

This document defines the standard structure for institutional assurance reporting packs.

## Pack Sections

1. Reporting context
2. Audience and classification
3. Evidence validity status
4. Governance posture
5. Allocation posture
6. Scenario and reserve posture
7. Portfolio confidence summary
8. Open escalations and exceptions
9. Disclosure limitations
10. Validity and approval statement

## Pack Rule

Each section must be tied to:

- a validated upstream layer
- a defined disclosure boundary
- a traceable rationale
- a current validity state

## Invalid Pack Conditions

An assurance pack is invalid if:

- any required upstream validity layer is broken
- classification is missing
- exclusions are omitted
- escalation status is hidden
- disclosure exceeds approved boundary

## Pack Outcome

The assurance pack must convert internal governed state into an externally credible, bounded, and institutionally defensible reporting artifact.
