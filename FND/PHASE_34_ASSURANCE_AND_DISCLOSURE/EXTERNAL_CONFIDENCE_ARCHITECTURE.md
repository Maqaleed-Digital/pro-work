# EXTERNAL CONFIDENCE ARCHITECTURE

## Purpose

This document defines how WorkCaptain / ProWork builds external confidence through governed reporting and disclosure architecture.

## Confidence Principle

External confidence must be constructed from governed truth, not presentation polish.

Confidence is earned through:

- evidence lineage
- governance discipline
- allocation discipline
- resilience discipline
- bounded disclosure
- explicit limitation handling

## Confidence Layers

### Layer 1 — Internal truth integrity
Evidence and governance must remain valid.

### Layer 2 — Institutional reporting integrity
Assurance packs must remain classification-correct and validity-correct.

### Layer 3 — External confidence integrity
Investor and partner confidence must be based only on what is safe, true, and governable to disclose.

## Confidence Failure Conditions

External confidence is degraded when:

- disclosure outruns evidence
- narrative outruns governance
- posture is overstated
- exclusions are hidden
- escalation is omitted
- limitations are obscured

## Architecture Outcome

The external confidence architecture should enable:

- stronger investor trust
- stronger strategic partner confidence
- more credible board-to-external alignment
- institutionally defensible reporting posture
