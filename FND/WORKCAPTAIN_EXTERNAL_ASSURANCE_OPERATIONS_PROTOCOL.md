# WORKCAPTAIN / PROWORK — EXTERNAL ASSURANCE OPERATIONS PROTOCOL

Version: 1.0
Status: ACTIVE

## Purpose

Standardize how the platform is represented to external stakeholders after certification.

## Stakeholder Classes

- Board / Executive
- Investors
- Enterprise Clients
- Sovereign / Strategic Partners

## Protocol Rules

1. Use only approved Phase 24 and Phase 25 artifacts
2. Do not exceed declared assurance boundary
3. Do not imply regulator endorsement
4. Do not imply legal opinion
5. Do not imply third-party audit accreditation unless separately obtained
6. Map every assurance response to named artifacts
7. Preserve versioned evidence continuity

## Required Response Basis

All external statements must be traceable to:

- governance seal
- board readiness pack
- enterprise assurance profile
- sovereign declaration
- institutional deployment readiness pack
- assurance disclosure boundary
