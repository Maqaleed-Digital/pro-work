# PHASE 117 — VERTICALIZATION LAYER

## OBJECTIVE
Create industry-specific operating models so the platform becomes category-relevant, higher-retention, and harder to replace.

## CORE OUTCOME
Shift from broad capability platform to vertical-ready operating infrastructure.

## TARGET VERTICAL MODEL

### 1. Consulting / Professional Services
- client engagement workflows
- proposal-to-delivery lifecycle
- milestone governance
- expert review checkpoints
- delivery evidence packs

### 2. Fintech / Credit Operations
- application intake flows
- review and risk scoring workflows
- collections and follow-up playbooks
- regulated approval checkpoints
- trust and audit logging

### 3. Industrial / Fertilizer / Supply Chain
- procurement coordination flows
- supplier onboarding workflows
- quality and compliance checkpoints
- capacity and inventory readiness signals
- approval-driven operational response

## VERTICAL COMPONENTS
- vertical policy packs
- vertical data fields
- vertical playbook bundles
- vertical KPI sets
- vertical dashboard presets
- vertical evidence templates

## EVENTS
VERTICAL_PROFILE_DEFINED
VERTICAL_POLICY_PACK_ASSIGNED
VERTICAL_PLAYBOOK_BUNDLE_ACTIVATED
VERTICAL_KPI_PROFILE_ASSIGNED

## GOVERNANCE
- each vertical must define explicit workflow boundaries
- policy differences must be configuration-based
- vertical logic must remain tenant-safe and versioned
