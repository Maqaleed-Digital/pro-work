# WORKCAPTAIN / PROWORK — REVENUE DECISION AND ESCALATION WORKFLOW

Version: 1.0
Status: ACTIVE

## Workflow

1. confirm stage
2. confirm owner
3. confirm forecast posture
4. confirm evidence basis
5. confirm risk and blockage state
6. decide:
   - proceed
   - review
   - escalate
   - pause
   - park
   - close_won
   - close_lost

## Rule

No revenue decision may be logged without stage, owner, forecast posture, and evidence basis.
