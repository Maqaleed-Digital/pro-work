# WORKCAPTAIN / PROWORK — CERTIFICATION MAINTENANCE AND RENEWAL PROTOCOL

Version: 1.0
Status: ACTIVE

## Purpose

Define how certified status is maintained, reviewed, and renewed.

## Maintenance Rules

- certification must be periodically reviewed
- evidence continuity must remain intact
- critical controls must remain active
- assurance artifacts must remain current
- material governance changes require reassessment

## Renewal Triggers

- major architecture change
- critical policy change
- control degradation event
- evidence continuity break
- major deployment boundary change
- material assurance narrative change

## Renewal Outcomes

- maintained
- renewal_required
- blocked

## Rule

Certified state is not assumed permanent.
It must be actively maintained.
