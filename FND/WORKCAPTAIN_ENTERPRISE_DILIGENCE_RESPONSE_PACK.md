# WORKCAPTAIN / PROWORK — ENTERPRISE DILIGENCE RESPONSE PACK

Version: 1.0
Status: ACTIVE

## Purpose

Provide a standardized response framework for enterprise diligence.

## Diligence Themes Covered

- governance model
- authorization and approval controls
- evidence and auditability
- tenant and jurisdiction isolation
- compliance posture support
- assurance boundary
- certification maintenance discipline

## Response Rule

Every diligence response must cite approved platform artifacts and remain within the assurance disclosure boundary.
