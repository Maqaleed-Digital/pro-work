# WORKCAPTAIN / PROWORK — DEAL DECISION AND ESCALATION MODEL

Version: 1.0
Status: ACTIVE

## Decision Classes

- proceed
- hold
- escalate
- reject

## Escalation Triggers

- unsupported claim request
- boundary ambiguity
- material assurance challenge
- new regulatory question
- commitment request outside approved posture
- owner uncertainty on response basis

## Rule

Escalation must happen before response when assurance boundary or artifact basis is unclear.
